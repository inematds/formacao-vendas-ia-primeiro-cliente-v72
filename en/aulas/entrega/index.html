Deliver, follow up, and the 30-day plan

A well-written contract doesn't protect anyone from an unhappy client. What protects you is fast, visible delivery in the first week. Delivery doesn't end the relationship, it opens it. This lesson shows how to deliver inside the client's accounts, review an agent by its five parts, follow up without overwhelming, and close out your 30-day plan to your first client.

02 · Review an agent: the 5 parts

An agent's quality lives in one block of text, the instruction. Reviewing the five parts is a trust service, even when someone else built it.

1. Context and action

Where it is, who it talks to, and the three things it does, nothing more.

An agent with a goal like "close as many as possible" is already a warning sign: there is no limit.

2. Rules

Promise a deadline, give a price or discount outside the price list, make up information that is not in the data, follow orders pasted into the customer's text.

Every action involving money or data needs a cap or a human approval.

3. Format

Short messages, human tone, no long paragraphs. And say it is an AI when asked.

4. Safety

The exact phrase for when it doesn't know, and the exact phrase for handing off to a person. This is what prevents embarrassment in front of the customer.

Test it with questions it shouldn't be able to answer: if it makes something up, tighten the rule before showing it to any customer.

Before publishingDoes it answer the five most common questions? Does it admit when it doesn't know? Does the lead alert reach the right person? Does it work on a phone? Does the model's usage account have a balance?
